A neon station management game about building a transport hub that can take the pressure.

You design and operate a growing futuristic station: lay out the concourse, keep crowds of passengers moving, and hold your star rating high enough to unlock the next tier. Four systems pull against each other the whole way through.

Placement decides how the space is shaped.
Passenger flow reacts to that shape.
Station ratings grade the result live.
Progression gates what you are allowed to build next.

Every layout decision changes how passengers path through the station, which moves your ratings, which decides what unlocks. The loop closes on itself: a better rating pulls in bigger crowds, so succeeding is exactly what makes the next few minutes harder.

Passengers are not decoration. They enter, queue for ticket machines, pass through barriers and reach their platform in time for a train, hundreds at once, and they pick facilities by the lowest estimated total delay rather than by whichever one is nearest. A closer machine with six people at it will lose to a further one standing empty.

Around that core sit train scheduling, janitors and security drones that dispatch themselves, an economy with running costs as well as income, and saving so a station survives between sessions.


PLAYING IN THE BROWSER

Runs in the browser, no download. Your station saves automatically and is stored in your browser, so you can close the tab and pick it back up later. Clearing your site data will clear the save with it. There is a Windows build below if you would rather play it natively.

The fullscreen button in the bottom right corner is worth using. It is a dense interface and it benefits from the room.


CONTROLS

WASD, or drag with right mouse: pan the camera
Q and E: rotate the camera
Scroll wheel: zoom
R, or right click: rotate the piece you are placing
Left click: place and select
Escape: close the open menu
Y: hide the interface for a clean look at your station


ABOUT
